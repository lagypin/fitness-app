## Done in version 2.1

- A Short, 30, or 45 picker for any single day on the Today tab, with easy cardio filling leftover minutes.

- Planned aerobic minutes on the Week tab, next to the 150-minute target.
- CSV export as two files, Sessions and Sets, with a data dictionary in the README.

## Done in version 2.2

- Thursday, Saturday, and Sunday are open days. The mobility flows and the Sunday Surprise are gone.
- The foot-care add-on is gone.
- Lift days warm up with an easy row and lighter sets of the lifts, and cool down with one stretch per lift. The cool-down counts toward the session.
- Four tabs: Today, Week, Log, More. Settings, standing swaps, Your data, the Library, and the version live under More.
- Why is a standalone page at /why, built from the same evidence file as the app.
- "Days moved this week" replaces the streak.
- The screen stays on from the Start tap, with nosleep.js as the fallback and a status line that says which.
- The rest timer ends with a chime and a "Rest's up" bar.
- A finish card with a cheer, and sometimes a fact from the log, after every saved session.
